What your label and your bands get

  • A written audio audit per track: frequency balance against Black, Death, Doom and Gothic references, dynamics and loudness, stereo image and phase. Your bands see why each decision was made.
  • One consistent sound across your catalog: loudness and tone stay comparable from release to release, so a label sampler or playlist does not jump in level.
  • Album consistency: EPs and albums get loudness balance across tracks and optimized sequencing and transitions.
  • Every format: 24-bit WAV, 16-bit AIFF and 320 kbps MP3, with exports for Spotify, Apple Music and Bandcamp. Vinyl-ready master on request for albums.
  • Unlimited revisions: no extra cost, no time limit, on every release.

How label mastering works

  1. Tell us your release schedule. A short email with your label, genres and how many releases you plan per year is enough to unlock partner rates.
  2. Send the mixes per release. Stereo mixdowns or stems, plus one or two reference tracks from the band's subgenre.
  3. Audit, then master. Each track is analysed first; the written report and the master are delivered together.
  4. Revise until it fits. The band or the label gives specific feedback, and the master is reworked until it is right.
  5. Pay per release. Prepaid or on invoice after delivery.

Label mastering FAQ

Who gets label partner rates?
Independent labels with a release schedule of at least 4 releases per year. The rates are fixed: €63 per stereo single, €103 per stem single, €279 per EP (up to 5 tracks) and €499 per album (up to 10 tracks). All prices are final prices incl. 19% German VAT.
How does payment work for labels?
Each release is either prepaid or invoiced after delivery, whichever suits your label. There is no subscription and no recurring fee; you pay per release at the partner rate.
What if we release fewer than 4 records a year?
Then the regular packages apply: €79 per stereo single, €129 per stem single, €349 for an EP and €629 for an album. Artists with several singles can book a Single Series of 3 singles at €73 each. The audit and unlimited revisions are included either way.
Do label releases get the same audit?
Yes. Every track gets the same written audio audit as a regular order: frequency balance against genre references, dynamics and loudness, stereo image and phase. Same process, same unlimited revisions, only the price per release is lower.
Can you deliver vinyl and Bandcamp masters?
Yes. Every master comes as 24-bit WAV, 16-bit AIFF and 320 kbps MP3 with exports for Spotify, Apple Music and Bandcamp. A separate vinyl-ready master is available on request for album projects.
Which genres do you master?
Black Metal, Death Metal, Doom and Gothic, plus Metalcore and Djent. Polished works only with heavy and dark music, so loudness and tone are judged against references from your subgenre, not against pop masters.
