How Suno Mastering works

Here's how it'll work from January 2027:

  1. Your file: a WAV, AIFF or FLAC export of your song (44.1–96 kHz). You confirm that your plan on the AI tool allows commercial use.
  2. Your genre: you pick the profile closest to your song. Each profile is the measured spread of released records: tonal balance, loudness, dynamics, stereo width and harshness.
  3. The master: clipped peaks rebuilt, rumble removed, tonal balance moved into the genre range (only what lies outside it), low end centred, level set by dynamics with a true-peak ceiling of -1 dBTP.
  4. Quality checks: every master is measured again for true peak, clipping, dynamics, tonal balance, harshness and mono compatibility. A master that fails a check isn't delivered.
  5. Delivery: WAV at 16 bit / 44.1 kHz (the format distributors ask for), MP3 and a PDF report with the numbers before and after.

Genre profiles: metal, dark and electronic

We measured the profiles from released records, with the same measurement we run on your file:

  • Black Metal (atmospheric / post)
  • Depressive / raw Black Metal
  • Melodic Death Metal
  • Brutal Death / Grind
  • Doom / Gothic Metal
  • Modern / Industrial Metal, NDH, Nu Metal
  • Psytrance
  • Hitech / Psycore / Darkpsy
  • Dubstep / Bass Music
  • Hardcore / Hardstyle

Pop, hip-hop and singer-songwriter songs don't have a profile yet. We'd rather tell you that than guess.

AI music in 2026: the numbers

Deezer receives almost 75,000 fully AI-generated tracks a day, about 44% of all daily uploads (Deezer press release, 20 April 2026). In June 2026 the share passed 50% at its peak. Yet AI tracks are only between 1 and 3% of all streams on Deezer, and in 2025 up to 85% of the streams of fully AI-generated tracks were fraudulent. Deezer tags these tracks and removes them from algorithmic recommendations.

What this means for your song: mastering doesn't change how a platform classifies it, and we never try to hide where a track comes from. What a master can change is how your song holds up next to everything else in a playlist: level, harshness, low end and stereo image.

What Suno Mastering doesn't do

  • It doesn't listen. It measures and follows rules from real releases; the taste is yours.
  • It doesn't fix the song itself: a buried vocal, smeared cymbals or a muddy arrangement stay the way they are. The report names these problems, so you can regenerate or edit those parts.
  • It doesn't hide AI origin, and it won't change how Deezer, Spotify or anyone else detects or labels your track.
  • No stems, no vinyl master, no album matching and no reference track of your own.

Suno Mastering FAQ

What is Suno mastering?
Suno mastering is the final step for a song made with Suno or another AI music tool before release: level, tonal balance, low end, stereo image and peaks are brought in line with released records of the genre. Suno Mastering at Polished does this automatically and measures every master against real releases.
Does mastering make my AI track pass as human-made?
No. Mastering changes level and tone, not where a song comes from. Deezer tags fully AI-generated tracks and removes them from algorithmic recommendations, and Suno Mastering doesn't try to get around that.
Which genres work best?
Metal, gothic and dark music, psytrance, hitech and other electronic styles, because we measured the genre profiles from released records in those genres. Pop, hip-hop and singer-songwriter songs don't have a profile yet.
Do I need the rights to my Suno song?
Yes. You confirm that your plan on the AI tool allows commercial use of the song. Free plans of AI music tools are often limited to personal use, so check your tool's terms.
When does Suno Mastering start?
In January 2027, together with Polished Auto. If you're on the waitlist, you'll get one message when it opens.
