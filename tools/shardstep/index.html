Shardstep: tempo-synced stutter and glitch for 200+ BPM

Shardstep chops, repeats, reverses and tape-stops audio in time with your DAW, with a short crossfade on every slice edge so nothing clicks. Tim Borchert's label Code Chaos Audio made it for hitech and psycore at 200 to 300 BPM. For bands it fits industrial and electronic crossover songs, breakdown fills and transitions where a guitar or drum bus should stutter on the grid.

Glitch edits that survive mastering

At 250 BPM a 1/32 repeat lasts 30 ms and a 1/64 only 15 ms. Cut audio that short without a fade and every slice edge clicks. In the mix those clicks are easy to miss; after mastering, when the limiter has raised everything, they become audible and they eat headroom like extra transients.

  • Slice Fade puts a raised-cosine crossfade from 0.5 to 8 ms on every slice edge and every retrigger. Longer fades are smoother, shorter ones snappier.
  • Untouched when idle: when nothing plays (transport stopped, empty pattern, mix at 0 % or a bar the pattern skips), the audio passes through bit for bit. The rest of the song keeps its sound.
  • Zero latency: repeats replay audio that has already arrived, so the track stays in time with the rest of the mix.

One practical habit: render or freeze the stutter track before you send stems or a mix to anyone. A pattern that depends on the DAW transport and bar position is easy to change by accident.

Using it in a band mix

Patterns play every bar or only on the last bar of 2, 4 or 8, which is how fills and drops work. Hold a MIDI note to fire an effect by hand (C = 1/8 up to A# = Crush, any octave); a held note overrides the pattern and also works with the transport stopped. Among the 15 factory patterns is one called Bar 8 Breakdown Drop.

Short repeats of a kick (1/64) are shorter than one cycle of the kick and can sit slightly off centre while they play. The signal returns to your untouched audio as soon as the effect ends.

Shardstep FAQ

Does Shardstep run on a Mac?
Yes, on Apple Silicon Macs (arm64) as VST3 and Standalone. There is no Intel build and no AU version, so Logic Pro is not supported. The macOS build is not signed or notarized, so you allow it once under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. The Shardstep demo on Gumroad is the full plugin with a short noise burst every 45 seconds.
Does Shardstep work below 200 BPM?
Yes. The pattern follows whatever tempo your DAW sends; the automated tests also run at 150 BPM. It was designed with very fast tempos in mind, where clicks are the biggest problem.
