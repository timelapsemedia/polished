Chaos FM: 3-operator FM synth for electronic layers

Chaos FM is a three-operator FM synth with a GENERATE button that rolls a new, playable patch for the category you pick: lead, bass, acid, stab, pluck or FX. Code Chaos Audio, Tim Borchert's plugin label, built it for hitech and psycore. For bands it is a quick source of metallic leads, synth bass layers and stabs in industrial or electronic crossover songs. Windows only.

FM synths in a guitar mix, before mastering

FM sounds are bright and dense by nature, and in a metal mix they compete with cymbals and guitar fizz for the same upper range. A few habits keep them from becoming the harshest part of the master:

  • Use the filter. Chaos FM has a zero-delay lowpass with envelope amount and drive. Closing it a little takes the edge off before any EQ is needed.
  • Check unison in mono. Up to four unison voices with stereo spread sound wide on headphones; detuned voices can thin out when the mix is summed to mono.
  • Keep synth bass and kick apart. In mono/legato mode with glide, a synth bass can follow the bass guitar. Check its relationship to the kick with the free Kick/Bass Scope.
  • Save what GENERATE gives you. A rolled patch is gone once you roll again. Save it or print the part to audio before the mix goes out.

How to hear it first

There is no separate audio demo on this page. The quickest way to hear Chaos FM in your own song is the free demo on Gumroad: the full synth, with a short noise burst every 45 seconds. It runs on Windows only.

Chaos FM FAQ

Does Chaos FM run on a Mac?
No. Chaos FM is a Windows 10/11 plugin (VST3 and Standalone).
Is there a free trial?
Yes. The Chaos FM demo on Gumroad is the full synth with presets and GENERATE; it plays a short noise burst every 45 seconds.
What does the GENERATE button do?
It creates a new patch for the category you pick (Lead, Bass, Acid, Stab, Pluck or FX), built to stay musically usable instead of random noise.
