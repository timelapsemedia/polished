Kick/Bass Scope: see whether kick and bass cancel, for free

Kick/Bass Scope is a free VST3 analyzer that draws your kick and your bass on top of each other, locked to your DAW's beat, and shows whether they add up or cancel each other. Tim Borchert built it under his Code Chaos Audio label for fast electronic music. The problem it shows is the same in a death metal or metalcore mix: two low-end sources fighting over the same moment.

Why kick/bass phase matters before mastering

Kick and bass share the lowest octave of a metal mix. Where they overlap, their waves either add up or partly cancel, and both cases change what the master can do:

  • Cancelling: the low end loses energy exactly on the beat. Mastering EQ can only raise kick and bass together, which also raises everything that was already fine. The fix belongs in the mix: flip the bass polarity, shift the bass notes or adjust the kick sample. The scope's warning text suggests exactly that.
  • Adding up: the sum gets louder than either part, and in mastering the limiter grabs those moments first. The scope shows the peak of kick, bass and sum per window in dBFS against an adjustable clip line, so you see how much headroom the low end really takes.

In metal the relationship changes every time the chain changes: a new kick sample, a different bass amp or cab, an extra filter. Check again after each of those, not only once at the start of the mix.

Routing in two minutes

  1. Put the scope on the bass track.
  2. Send the kick to its sidechain input.
  3. If your DAW cannot feed a sidechain into an effect, switch Input to Single input: route the kick panned hard left and the bass hard right to one bus and put the scope there.
  4. Pick a window of 1/4, 1/2, 1 bar or 2 bars. It follows the host tempo and bar position, and keeps running freely when the transport is stopped.

Your audio passes through untouched: the output is bit-for-bit identical to the input. That is why there is no audio demo on this page, there is nothing to hear. The two screenshots show what it measures.

Kick/Bass Scope FAQ

Is Kick/Bass Scope really free?
Yes. It is a pay-what-you-want product on Gumroad; you can enter 0 € and download it.
Does it run on a Mac?
Yes, on Apple Silicon Macs (arm64) as VST3 and Standalone. There is no Intel build and no AU version, so Logic Pro is not supported. The macOS build is not signed or notarized, so you allow it once under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
My DAW cannot route a sidechain into an effect. Can I still use it?
Yes. Set Input to Single input, pan the kick hard left and the bass hard right on one bus and put the scope on that bus.
