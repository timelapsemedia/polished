Tools for bands · Code Chaos Audio

Tools for bands, built next to the mastering desk.

Many problems a mastering engineer finds start much earlier: a fizzy amp tone, a reverb that collapses in mono, a kick and bass that cancel each other. Tim Borchert, who masters every track at Polished, also builds audio plugins under the label Code Chaos Audio. Here they are with screenshots, real demo audio and plain system requirements. Everything is sold on Gumroad, and most plugins have a free demo.

Where each tool fits

/ 01

Writing and demos

Frostfret plays guitar and bass from MIDI, Hoarfell plays drums, bass and a choir pad. Both are modelled, with no samples, and made for demos and pre-production.

/ 02

Tone

Gravesaw turns guitar and bass DIs into a black metal amp and cab sound, with 19 built-in cabs.

/ 03

Space

Rimehall adds a cold hall, shimmer and tape, and ducks the reverb while the riff plays.

/ 04

Low end

Kick/Bass Scope shows whether kick and bass add up or cancel. It is free.

/ 05

Saturation

Crucible saturates bass, drums and buses in three bands with five saturation types per band.

/ 06

Electronic layers

Shardstep adds tempo-synced stutter and glitch fills, Chaos FM generates FM leads, basses and stabs.

Tools for bands: FAQ

Who makes these plugins?
Tim Borchert, the mastering engineer behind Polished, builds them under the label Code Chaos Audio. Polished links to them; buying and downloading happens on Gumroad.
Do the plugins run on a Mac?
Gravesaw, Rimehall, Frostfret, Hoarfell, Shardstep and Kick/Bass Scope run on Apple Silicon Macs as VST3 and Standalone. There are no Intel builds and no AU versions, and the macOS builds are not signed or notarized. Crucible and Chaos FM are Windows only.
Can I try them before buying?
Yes. Gravesaw, Rimehall, Frostfret, Hoarfell, Shardstep, Crucible and Chaos FM each have a free demo on Gumroad: the full plugin with a short noise burst every 45 seconds. Kick/Bass Scope is free.
Do Frostfret and Hoarfell sound like real recordings?
They are modelled, with no samples. Distorted and acoustic guitars come closest to a recording, the clean electric guitar sounds the most synthetic. The drums are made for demos and pre-production, and the choir sings vowels only, as a pad behind the mix. Each page has demo audio to judge for yourself.
Is there a bundle?
Yes, two. Black Metal Suite Essentials (Gravesaw, Rimehall, Tone Kit, Drum Kit) costs €39 instead of €59. Black Metal Suite Complete adds Frostfret and Hoarfell and costs €69 instead of €105.
